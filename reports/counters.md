# Lane counters that hold up across seasons

For each lane matchup we compare actual wins with the wins expected from both champions' strength *in that season and role*. **Lift** is the extra win rate beyond that expectation; **z** is how many standard errors it is from zero (|z| > 2 is unlikely to be noise); **Seasons ahead** counts the seasons (with 3+ games) where the edge was positive. In fearless series, later games are down-weighted (G1 1.0 → G5 0.35) because they are drafted from leftover pools; win rate is the raw rate.

Only matchups with at least 20 pro games and champions still played in the current meta are listed. Full table: `reports/matchups.csv`.

## Caveats

- Pro samples are small: even 40 games leaves roughly ±8% uncertainty on a win rate. Use z and seasons, not lift alone.
- Reworks and item changes can break old matchups; check that the years column includes recent seasons.
- Draft context matters: a counter pick is often a last-pick decision, so it's partly selection bias (teams counter-pick when the matchup is favourable for reasons the data doesn't see).
