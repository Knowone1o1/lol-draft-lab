How the test works

Patches are drawn at random from 2026 and split into tuning and validation sets. For each patch, several scenarios keep a random share of its games (0% to 80%) and delete the rest. Every statistic (champion strength, player comfort, counters, synergies, Elo) is rebuilt without the deleted games and without anything from later patches or dates. A logistic model then predicts each deleted game's winner, fitted on the other patches only. A coordinate search tries one weight at a time on the tuning patches and keeps a change only if it lowers log-loss; the validation patches check that the gain is real.
